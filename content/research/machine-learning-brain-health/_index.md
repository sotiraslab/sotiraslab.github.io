The MINDS Lab develops machine learning methods to study brain health across development, adulthood, and aging.

Our work spans:
- Brain age and development modeling
- Neurodegeneration and cognitive decline
- Psychiatric and neurodevelopmental variation
- Robust learning on heterogeneous imaging cohorts
