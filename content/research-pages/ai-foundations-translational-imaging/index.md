We build the methodological foundation for imaging AI, with emphasis on models that are reproducible, scalable, and useful in translational biomedical settings.

Our work includes:
- Representation learning and multimodal fusion
- Segmentation and reconstruction methods
- Scalable optimization for large imaging datasets
- Tools that move methods from research to practice
